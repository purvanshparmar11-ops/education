65+ Years of Educational Service

Nurturing Generations of Achievers in Odhav, Ahmedabad

Established in 1961, H. B. Mehta Vallabhnagar High School (along with R. J. Mehta Vallabhnagar Primary School) stands as one of the premier educational institutions in the Odhav area of Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

Affiliated with the Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Education Board (GSEB), our school provides government-aided education designed to empower students from all socio-economic backgrounds with rigorous academic skills, strong ethical values, and practical competence.

Our Vision

To inspire holistic academic mastery, character excellence, and social responsibility in every student.

Our Mission

Providing accessible, high-quality GSEB education through modern lab infrastructure and disciplined guidance.

Campus Infrastructure & Facilities

Creating an enriching environment for academic, practical, and physical development.

Science Laboratories

Dedicated Physics, Chemistry, and Biology laboratories equipped with modern apparatus for hands-on GSEB practical experiments.

Computer Laboratory

High-speed computer lab with internet access, providing practical IT education and digital literacy skills.

Library & Reading Room

Over 8,000 books, academic journals, GSEB reference materials, and quiet reading space for students and faculty.

Sports Playground

Spacious playground for athletics, cricket, volleyball, kabaddi, and annual sports events encouraging physical fitness.

NCC & Scout Unit

Active National Cadet Corps (NCC) and Scouts & Guides wing inculcating patriotic duty, leadership, and discipline.

Assembly Hall

Multi-purpose auditorium for morning prayers, cultural programs, inter-school debates, and guest lectures.
