School Sports Event

25th Annual Inter-House Athletic & Sports Championship

H. B. Mehta High School is proud to organize the 25th Annual Inter-House Sports Meet at the school athletic ground in Odhav. Students from Red, Blue, Green, and Yellow houses will compete in track & field events, team sports, and traditional Indian games.

Event Highlights & Disciplines

  • Athletics: 100m, 200m, 400m sprint, 4x100m relay race, shot put, and long jump.
  • Team Games: Inter-house Kabaddi, Volleyball, Badminton, and Chess tournaments.
  • NCC Parade: Flag hoisting and marching drill by H. B. Mehta School NCC Cadet Contingent.
  • Prize Ceremony: Trophies and medals presented by District Sports Officer and Principal Dr. Rajesh Sharma.

Schedule for Event Day

08:00 AM: March Past & Opening Ceremony
09:00 AM: Track & Field Heats
01:00 PM: Kabaddi & Volleyball Finals
03:30 PM: Valedictory & Trophy Presentation

Event Information

Date: 15th November 2026

Time: 08:00 AM - 04:00 PM

Location: Main Sports Ground, H. B. Mehta High School, Odhav, Ahmedabad

Coordinator: Physical Education Dept.
