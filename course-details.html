Higher Secondary Science & Commerce Streams Overview

H. B. Mehta Vallabhnagar High School provides a balanced blend of academic rigor, practical laboratory work, and continuous assessment aligned with the Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Education Board (GSEB) regulations.

1. Higher Secondary Science Stream (Class 11 & 12)

The Science Stream is designed for students seeking careers in Engineering, Medicine, Information Technology, Biotechnology, and Pure Sciences.

Group Core Subjects Practicals Career Avenues
Group A (PCM) Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, English, Computer Studies Physics Lab, Chemistry Lab, Computer Lab BE/B.Tech (JEE/GUJCET), Architecture, B.Sc. IT, Data Science
Group B (PCB) Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, Computer Studies Physics Lab, Chemistry Lab, Biology Lab MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BHMS, Pharmacy, Biotechnology (NEET)

2. Higher Secondary Commerce Stream (Class 11 & 12)

The Commerce Stream focuses on business management, accounting principles, financial economics, and commercial correspondence.

Subject Description Key Outcomes
Elements of Accounts Double entry system, ledger, trial balance, final accounts of sole trader and partnership. Foundation for CA Foundation & B.Com
Organization of Commerce (OCM) Business management principles, marketing, financial markets, and entrepreneurship. Foundation for BBA / MBA / Management
Economics & Statistics Micro and Macro economics, national income, statistical sampling, index numbers. Analytical data modeling & economic policy

Admission Eligibility & Documents Required

  • GSEB Class 10 (SSC) Original Marksheet & Passing Certificate.
  • School Leaving Certificate (LC) from previous school.
  • Aadhaar Card Copy & 4 Passport size photographs.
  • Caste / Income Certificate for scholarship eligibility (if applicable).
