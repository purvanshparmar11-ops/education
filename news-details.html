GSEB Official Circular

Important Guidelines for Class 10 (SSC) and Class 12 (HSC) Examination Candidates

Published: 10th August 2026 | By: GSEB Examination Committee, H. B. Mehta School

All students of Class 10 (Secondary) and Class 12 (Higher Secondary - Science & Commerce) appearing for Gujarat Secondary & Higher Secondary Education Board (GSEB) examinations are hereby notified of the upcoming preliminary schedule and guidelines:

Key Dates & Requirements

  • First Preliminary Exam: Commences on 20th September 2026. Attendance is mandatory for all students.
  • Board Registration Verification: Check spellings of student name, birth date, and subjects on draft hall tickets at the school administrative counter by 25th August 2026.
  • Science Practical Journals: Class 12 Science students must submit signed Physics, Chemistry, and Biology journals before 15th September.
  • Minimum Attendance: As per GSEB norms, 80% minimum attendance is compulsory for hall ticket issuance.

Doubt Solving & Extra Coaching Sessions

Special guidance classes in Mathematics, Science, Accounts, and English will be conducted every Saturday from 08:30 AM to 11:30 AM by senior GSEB faculty members.
