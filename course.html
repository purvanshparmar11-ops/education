GSEB Board Curriculum & Streams

Offering Gujarati & English medium streams with structured board examination preparation.

Std 5th to 8th

Primary Division (R. J. Mehta School)

Foundation academic education strengthening core concepts in Gujarati, English, Mathematics, Science, and Social Environment. Focus on activity-based learning and personality development.

  • Subjects: Gujarati, English, Hindi, Mathematics, Science, Social Science.
  • Medium: Gujarati & English Medium
  • Facilities: Activity hall, sports ground, drawing & music.
Detailed Curriculum
Std 9th & 10th (GSEB Board)

Secondary Division (GSEB SSC)

Rigorous secondary board education preparing students for the GSEB Class 10 Board Examinations with regular unit tests, doubt-solving sessions, and practical exam training.

  • Subjects: Science & Tech, Mathematics, Social Science, Languages, Computer.
  • Board: GSEB (Gujarat Secondary Board)
  • Feature: Mock prelim exams & personalized student counseling.
Detailed Curriculum
Std 11th & 12th (Science Stream)

Higher Secondary Science (GSEB HSC)

Specialized science stream offering Group A (Mathematics) and Group B (Biology) with intensive laboratory experiments, GUJCET coaching, and competitive exam guidance.

  • Core Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Biology / Mathematics, English, Computer.
  • Practicals: Fully equipped Physics, Chemistry & Biology labs.
  • Careers: Engineering (JEE/GUJCET), Medicine (NEET), Pharmacy, B.Sc.
Detailed Curriculum
Std 11th & 12th (Commerce Stream)

Higher Secondary Commerce (GSEB HSC)

Comprehensive financial, business, and accounting education designed for students aiming for Careers in Chartered Accountancy (CA), Management (BBA/MBA), and Commerce (B.Com).

  • Core Subjects: Accountancy, Economics, OCM, Statistics, SP & CC, English.
  • Specialization: Tally ERP & Computer Accounting awareness.
  • Careers: CA, CS, CMA, BBA, B.Com, Finance & Banking.
